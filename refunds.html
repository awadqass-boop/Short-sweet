Refunds & Cancellations

Because Short & Sweet products are made to order, cancellation and refund handling should depend on the stage of preparation and the circumstances of the order.

Before preparation

Contact Short & Sweet as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel an order. A request can only be confirmed after the team checks the order status.

After preparation begins

Once an order has entered preparation or customized packaging has started, cancellation or changes may not be possible.

Order issues

If there is an issue with an order received, contact Short & Sweet promptly with the order number and details so the matter can be reviewed.

No automatic refund promise is stated here because the final commercial refund rules have not yet been provided. Update this page with the exact approved policy before taking live payments.
