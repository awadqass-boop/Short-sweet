For more than 15 years, Khaliya has been part of my family’s Ramadan table. Our Salted Caramel Shorties carry another piece of family history — an Eid tradition started by my mother, with my own salted caramel twist.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll help.

How do I place an order?

Open the order page, choose your treats, sizes and flavours, then add your delivery or pickup details. Your order is sent to our WhatsApp, and we confirm everything — including the final total and delivery — before we bake.

How early should I order?

Everything is made to order. Orders of 10–20 pieces need 48 hours’ notice, orders above 20 pieces need 5 days, and customised orders need 7 days.

Do you offer delivery and pickup?

Yes, both. Choose your preference while ordering — delivery details and any delivery fee are confirmed with your order.

Which flavours can I choose?

Glass bowls and Shortie Pop Jars come in Coconut Cream (dark, white or mixed chocolate), Salted Pistachio and Salted Caramel. For a set of two or three bowls, you can pick a different flavour for each bowl.

Can I place a custom order?

Yes. Add “Something else” on the order page or message us on WhatsApp for celebrations, seasonal gifting or customised packaging, so we can confirm availability and timing.

What about allergens, storage and shelf life?

Please contact us before ordering if you need allergen, ingredient, storage or shelf-life information — we’re happy to help.
