Privacy Policy

This page explains how Short & Sweet may handle information submitted through this website when online ordering is enabled.

Information you provide

Order information may include your name, phone number, email address, delivery or pickup notes, preferred date/time, gift details, the items ordered, and order status information.

How it is used

The information is used to process and manage orders, communicate about an order, provide order tracking, and respond to customer requests.

Payments

Short & Sweet does not ask customers to enter raw card details directly into this website. Card payments should be handled by the selected secure payment provider once connected.

Analytics

Optional site analytics may be enabled later to understand website usage. If enabled, the site configuration should identify the analytics service in use.

Data sharing

Order information should only be shared with service providers where needed to fulfil the order, such as payment or delivery providers, subject to the arrangements in place with those providers.

This is a general website policy draft and should be reviewed before commercial launch to ensure it matches the final payment, delivery, analytics and data-handling setup.
