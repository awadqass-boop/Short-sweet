Terms of Use & Ordering

These terms are a general framework for ordering from Short & Sweet through the website.

Orders

An order request is subject to availability and confirmation by Short & Sweet. Preferred delivery/pickup dates and times are requests until confirmed.

Lead times

Orders between 10–20 pieces require 24-hour notice. Orders above 20 pieces require 48 hours. Customized packaging requires 7 days’ notice.

Pricing & payment

Displayed prices are in AED. Payment status is shown in the order tracker once payment is confirmed. Any active promotion must be specifically confirmed by Short & Sweet.

Custom orders

Custom packaging, celebration orders and special requests are subject to confirmation, timing and availability.

Product information

Customers with allergy, ingredient, storage or shelf-life questions should contact Short & Sweet before ordering.

This is a general terms draft, not legal advice. Review it before launch and update it to match your final operating and payment rules.
