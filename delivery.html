Ordering & delivery

Delivery & Pickup

Short & Sweet offers delivery and pickup. Customers choose their preferred method on the order page.

Delivery details

Delivery location, timing, availability and any applicable delivery fee are confirmed with the order. A preferred delivery time is not guaranteed until confirmed.

Pickup

Pickup is in Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Abu Dhabi. The exact location and pickup timing are shared when the order is confirmed.

Lead times

All orders are pre-orders. Orders of 10–20 pieces need 48 hours’ notice, orders above 20 pieces need 5 days’ notice, and customised orders need 7 days’ notice.

Order updates

Every order is confirmed with you on WhatsApp, and we keep you updated there until your treats are delivered or ready for pickup.
