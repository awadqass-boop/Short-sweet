Our story

Sweet traditions. Homemade happiness.

Short & Sweet Home Baked Treats began long before it became a business.

Some of the sweetest memories begin in the kitchen, with the scent of something baking, familiar flavours filling the air and loved ones waiting nearby for the first taste.

For more than 15 years, Khaliya has been part of my family’s Ramadan table. Our soft, comforting cheese balls began as a simple act of love and slowly grew into a tradition cherished by family and friends.

Our Salted Caramel Shorties carry another piece of family history. Originally an Eid tradition started by my mother, I took over making them more than a decade ago, adding my own salted caramel twist while keeping the heart of her recipe alive.

Long before it had a name, Short & Sweet Home Baked Treats was already taking shape through these treasured traditions.
