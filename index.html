Good to know

FAQ.

Do you offer delivery and pickup?

Yes. Both delivery and pickup are available. Choose your preference while ordering; delivery details and any applicable fee are confirmed with the order.

How early should I order?

Orders between 10–20 pieces require 24-hour notice, orders above 20 pieces require 48 hours, and customized packaging requires 7 days’ notice.

Can I place a custom order?

Yes. Use WhatsApp for celebrations, seasonal gifting or customized packaging so availability and timing can be confirmed.

How do I track my order?

After your order is created, use the order number and the same phone number you used at checkout on the Track Order page.

What about allergens, storage and shelf life?

Please contact Short & Sweet before ordering if you need allergen, ingredient, storage or shelf-life information. We do not publish assumptions about these details.
